**Last updated:** 2026-06-17
**Status:** DRAFT. Requires counsel review before publication.

## The short version

We use two cookies. Both are strictly necessary for the service to work. There is nothing to opt out of beyond the service itself. We have no advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or third-party tracking of any kind.

---

## What cookies we use

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `session` | Strictly necessary | Keeps you logged in | Session, or 30 days if you select "stay logged in" |
| `csrf_token` | Strictly necessary | Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks | Session |

## What we do not use

- Analytics cookies (no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no similar)
- Advertising or targeting cookies
- Social media tracking pixels
- Third-party cookies of any kind

## Why so few cookies

Our privacy architecture minimizes data collection by design. Third-party analytics would create tracking vectors that contradict our core privacy commitment. We chose not to use them.

## Your choices

Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled without breaking the service. Because we use no other cookies, there is no cookie preference manager — there is nothing to manage.

You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings. This will log you out.
