How private compute works

The architecture is the guarantee.

Two halves of one promise — fund anonymously, compute privately. Not a policy you trust; a path we’re built not to take. The whole mechanism, in the open.

front half Anonymous credit — pay without being identified
Your wallet
  • deposit ETH or tokens
  • a note is generated in your browser
  • nullifier + secret — never shared
deposit
Anonymity set
  • a Tornado-style Merkle set
  • your deposit joins the crowd
  • wallet ⇸ note link severed
ZK proof
Funded account
  • spend with a zero-knowledge proof
  • credits with no trace to you
  • we never learn your wallet
back half Private compute — work without being seen
Your device
  • WireGuard keypair generated in-browser
  • private key never leaves the device
  • prompts & results live only here
encrypted tunnel
Sealed GPU pod
  • confidential compute — SEV-SNP / TDX
  • your model runs inside the enclave
  • pod terminates · nothing persisted
